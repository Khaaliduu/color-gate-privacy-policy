Privacy Policy

COLOR GATE

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how COLOR GATE, a mobile game published by Khaalid Codecraft ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the game.

In short: COLOR GATE is designed to work offline. It does not require an account or an internet connection, and we do not use advertising or analytics services in the game.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, request, or receive personal information from players through COLOR GATE. The game does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

2. Internet Access, Advertising, and Analytics

COLOR GATE is intended to be playable offline. It does not use online account services, advertising networks, or analytics services. We do not use the game to track players across apps or websites.

3. How Information Is Used and Shared

Because we do not collect personal information through the game, we do not use or sell players' personal information, and we do not share it with third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

COLOR GATE does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other players. The game does not provide an account registration or personal-information submission feature.

5. Data Security and Retention

We do not maintain a server-side database of players' personal information collected through COLOR GATE. Any information handled by your device or the Android operating system is subject to your device's settings and platform controls.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game or its data practices change. Any updated version will be published at this same URL, with a revised effective date.
